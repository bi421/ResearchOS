# ResearchOS — Constitution

## Article II: Scope

### 2.1 What ResearchOS Does

ResearchOS performs exactly three functions:

#### 2.1.1 Market Intelligence Production

The system gathers, processes, and analyzes market data across multiple dimensions:

- **Macro Research:** Interest rates, monetary policy, geopolitical events, fiscal developments, and global liquidity conditions.
- **Technical Structure:** Market regime identification, trend analysis, support/resistance mapping, volatility profiling, and volume dynamics.
- **Liquidity Analysis:** Order flow interpretation, depth-of-market assessment, institutional positioning, and liquidity regime changes.
- **Cross-Market Relationships:** Intermarket analysis (equities, fixed income, currencies, commodities), correlation shifts, divergence detection, and capital flow tracking.
- **Scenario Generation:** Construction of probabilistic market scenarios based on current conditions, historical analogs, and regime-conditional outcomes.

#### 2.1.2 Research Validation

The system validates its own research outputs:

- **Reality Comparison:** Comparing past research scenarios with actual market outcomes.
- **Quality Scoring:** Quantitative assessment of research accuracy, timeliness, and relevance.
- **Failure Analysis:** Systematic identification of why research was wrong — whether due to missing data, flawed assumptions, model limitations, or cognitive errors.
- **Confidence Calibration:** Ensuring probability estimates are well-calibrated against observed frequencies.

#### 2.1.3 Human Cognitive Growth

The system helps the trader improve:

- **Reasoning Quality Evaluation:** Assessing the logical structure, completeness, and rigor of the trader's decision process.
- **Knowledge Growth Tracking:** Measuring expansion of the trader's mental models and market understanding over time.
- **Cognitive Bias Detection:** Identifying systematic patterns in the trader's thinking that lead to suboptimal decisions.
- **Decision Improvement:** Providing structured feedback and targeted exercises to strengthen weak areas.

### 2.2 What ResearchOS Never Does

These boundaries are absolute and non-negotiable:

1. **No trade execution.** ResearchOS never sends orders to any broker or exchange.
2. **No buy/sell signals.** The system does not produce "BUY" or "SELL" recommendations.
3. **No automated trading.** The system has no integration with any execution platform.
4. **No override of human judgment.** The human trader always retains final authority.
5. **No portfolio management.** The system does not allocate capital, size positions, or manage risk.
6. **No market timing alerts.** The system does not push notifications to enter or exit trades.
7. **No financial advice.** ResearchOS is a research tool, not a financial advisor.

### 2.3 Boundaries of Responsibility

| Responsibility | Human Trader | ResearchOS |
|---|---|---|
| Final trading decision | ✅ Always | ❌ Never |
| Research generation | ✅ Define questions | ✅ Produce analysis |
| Research validation | ✅ Review | ✅ Execute |
| Cognitive improvement | ✅ Apply feedback | ✅ Generate feedback |
| Risk management | ✅ Full authority | ❌ No authority |
| Execution | ✅ Sole authority | ❌ No access |
| Data sourcing | ✅ Source selection | ✅ Processing |
