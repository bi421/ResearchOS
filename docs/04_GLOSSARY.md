# ResearchOS — Constitution

## Article IV: Glossary

### 4.1 Core Terms

**ResearchOS**
The research operating system. A structured framework for generating, validating, and learning from institutional-grade market research.

**Human Trader**
The principal decision-maker. The human who makes all final trading decisions and whose cognitive growth is the ultimate goal of the system.

**Research**
A systematic investigation that follows the scientific method, produces falsifiable hypotheses, and generates conclusions that can be validated against reality.

**Market Intelligence**
Actionable knowledge about market conditions, structure, and probable future states, derived from systematic analysis of empirical data.

**Research Validation**
The process of comparing past research outputs with actual market outcomes to measure quality, identify errors, and calibrate confidence.

**Cognitive Growth**
Measurable improvement in the trader's reasoning quality, knowledge depth, bias awareness, and decision-making capability over time.

### 4.2 Research Concepts

**Scenario**
A probabilistic description of a possible future market state, including the conditions under which it would be considered valid or invalid.

**Hypothesis**
A falsifiable statement about market behavior that generates specific, testable predictions.

**Confidence Calibration**
The degree to which the system's probability estimates match observed frequencies. A well-calibrated system's 70% predictions are correct 70% of the time.

**Research Quality Score**
A quantitative measure of research accuracy, timeliness, completeness, and relevance.

**Failure Mode**
A category of error that caused research to be incorrect, such as data error, assumption error, model error, or cognitive error.

### 4.3 Cognitive Concepts

**Cognitive Bias**
A systematic pattern of deviation from rational judgment, such as confirmation bias, anchoring, or overconfidence.

**Reasoning Quality**
A measure of the logical structure, completeness, and rigor of a decision process, independent of the outcome.

**Mental Model**
A trader's internal representation of how markets work. Knowledge growth is measured as improvement in mental model accuracy.

**Bias Detection**
The identification of cognitive biases in the trader's reasoning process through structured analysis of decision patterns.

### 4.4 Technical Concepts

**Multi-Factor Analysis**
Research that integrates evidence from multiple independent analytical dimensions before reaching a conclusion.

**Regime Identification**
The classification of current market conditions into a defined market regime (e.g., trending, ranging, volatile, illiquid).

**Cross-Market Relationship**
A statistical or structural relationship between two or more different markets (e.g., USD and commodities, bonds and equities).

**Look-Ahead Bias**
An error in which analysis uses data that would not have been available at the time the research was conducted.

**Falsifiability**
The property of a statement being capable of being proven false by empirical evidence. Unfalsifiable statements are not research.
